Every concept here is filed by **what kind of page it is**: the directory carries its nature,
and the [index](../docs/index.md) lists the sections in the order the ideas build. That leaves
no room for the other question a reader asks, which is what a page is *about*, so the subject
rides on tags instead. The reasoning is
[Split orthogonal classification axes across folders and tags](../docs/knowledge_management/split_orthogonal_classification_axes_across_folders_and_tags.md).

This page is the other way in. Every tag in use, and every page under it, so a subject that
runs across six directories can be read as one thing.
